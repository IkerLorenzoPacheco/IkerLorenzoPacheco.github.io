Aimed Persona:
Job Interviewer, seeks people interested in working with tech and willing to give their 100% to accomplish the given tasks. Wants to know how capable someone is in order to determine the person's compatibility with the company's demands. Interested in how used you are to modern, constantly changing technologies as well as your ability to understand any given information. Needs a visual conformation of how you structure a solution's components and present it to the reader. Furthermore, he considers a way of contacting such candidates on a daily basis during the selection process.

Content Distribution: 
I based the page's main structure based on my previously finished CV. As I've gained experience working during the last two years, I realized someone's flexibility to be in distinct work areas is just as important as your physical image- which is the main reason why I placed my professional experience as the reader's first contact with me. Nonetheless, I still wanted to remind him of how much time I've spent under academic training before being put under any considerable responsibility. Taking that into account, my academic titles are displayed under the previous part
After that, I intend to reinforce a positive impression of me using certifications (like a first-aid course and my material handling equipment driving license) that show several skills that might not have been noticed on the first half of this section. To end the rush of paper-written talents, I've put a simple list of the languages I understand with ease as a simple finisher for this part. 

For the remaining data to show, I've decided to group together my contact info along with a brief description about myself. I thought about putting both my phone number and my email, as they're two of the fastest ways to conversate someone as well as exchanging data (wether it's orally or digitally).
